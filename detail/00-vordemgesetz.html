A man stands in front of a door. The gatekeeper does not let him through, but does not forbid him from doing so either. So he waits his whole life. Kafka’s parable of the gatekeeper encapsulates an experience we go through every day without consciously realising it : we hold our smartphone in our hand, the text is right in front of us, and yet we never quite reach it.

The installation “ Vor dem Gesetz ” translates Kafka’s parable into a digital reading experience. The visitor enters a doorway and becomes a character in the parable themselves. They stand before a door that changes shape. Instructions appear on it, giving the gatekeeper a voice. On the smartphone in their hand, the text eludes them and only becomes readable through interaction. Every gesture triggers a reaction in the space, yet access remains limited.

The work allows us to experience how digital interfaces determine what, when and how we read, and how our perceived control differs from the actual conditions of reading.
